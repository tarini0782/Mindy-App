# Research gap and novelty (candidate contribution — not a proven first)
**Gap suggested by the READ sources:** assessment games are built more than evaluated (Wiley 2021), and gamified child tools under-measure engagement (Bryant 2024).
**Candidate contribution:** a transparent, rule-based, evidence-linked observation layer over event-level play, with logged adaptive support and study arms, delivered with in-app child check-ins. Every cut-off is visible and replaceable.
**Not claimed:** first of its kind, clinical validity, accuracy, diagnosis, that adaptation helps. A literature search for prior art has been partial; novelty must be re-checked before the viva.
