# Support plan: how it works (v26)

Purpose: turn what a child tapped in Mindy into something a parent or caregiver can act on, or not.
It describes feelings the child REPORTED. It does not diagnose, predict, or explain causes.

## Inputs (all stored on the device, per child)
- Feeling check-ins before adventure activities (feed play and simulated-robot readings are not counted).
- Feeling after each activity (to see whether play helped).
- Worry picture scale answers before/after (prototype scale, not validated).
- Calm Corner visits (self-chosen).

## Band (last 14 days, needs at least 4 check-ins)
| Share of check-ins that were "scared" or "sad" | Band shown |
|---|---|
| fewer than 4 check-ins | Getting to know the child |
| below 30% | Mostly settled |
| 30% to under 60% | Some worried moments |
| 60% or more | Many worried moments lately |

## Signals (each shows its evidence and sample size)
Feelings summary; trend (last 7 days vs the 7 before, needs 3+ in each, gap of 25 points); settles after play
(low starts, last 30 days, needs 3+); best activity type (needs 2+ low starts per type and a clear gap);
worry faces change (needs 2+ pairs); worry high before last play; Calm Corner use; stopping early more often.

## Suggestions
| Id | Shown when | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| A_MORE_CHECKINS | not enough check-ins | Do now |
| A_KEEP_GOING | mostly settled | Do now |
| A_USE_WHAT_HELPS | one activity type clearly lifted the feeling after low starts | Do now |
| A_NAME_THE_FEELING | some or many worried moments | Do now |
| A_TRY_CALM_CORNER | worried moments and no Calm Corner use in 14 days | Do now |
| A_PRE_VISIT_ROUTINE | worried moments, or latest worry answer 2+ | This week |
| A_STEADY_ROUTINE | share of low check-ins rose vs last week | This week |
| A_CONSIDER_TALKING | many worried moments over 8+ check-ins across 7+ days, not improving, and play rarely helps; OR last two after-play worry answers both 3-4 | Worth considering |

The caregiver can choose "I'll try this" (a before/after description starts) or "Not now" (kept visible, marked).

## Limits, say these plainly
- Thresholds are engineering choices for a prototype. They are not clinical cut-offs and are not validated.
- Self-report from young children is noisy; a feeling can change for reasons unrelated to the game.
- The plan cannot say WHY a feeling appears or whether play caused a change.
- Whether following the suggestions reduces anxiety is a question for the ethics-approved pilot.
- Suggestions are general coping practice (naming feelings, slow breathing, practising before an event), not treatment.
