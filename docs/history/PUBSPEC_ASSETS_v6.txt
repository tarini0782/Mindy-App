Flutter only loads asset folders that are listed in pubspec.yaml (it does not
look inside sub-folders on its own). In C:\mindy_app\pubspec.yaml make the
"flutter:" section contain at least this (keep your existing lines too):

flutter:
  uses-material-design: true
  assets:
    - assets/audio/
    - assets/characters/kara/
    - assets/characters/nova/
    - assets/characters/lumi/
    - assets/characters/bubbles/
    - assets/characters/pip/
    - assets/characters/twinkle/
    - assets/worlds/magic_garden/
    - assets/worlds/bubble_valley/
    - assets/worlds/memory_castle/
    - assets/worlds/superhero_academy/
    - assets/worlds/rainbow_kingdom/
    - assets/worlds/dragon_valley/
    - assets/effects/
    - assets/sounds/
    - assets/music/

Dependencies needed (same as v5, nothing new): audioplayers,
flutter_local_notifications, image_picker. Then run: flutter pub get
